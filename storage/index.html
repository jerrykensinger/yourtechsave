Cloud storage and backup

What online storage really costs, and which kind you need.

Cloud storage and online backup sound alike but do different jobs. Start with the difference, then compare plans by size and cost per terabyte.

Start here

Cloud storage is not the same as backup.

Pick the job first. The right service depends on whether you want to reach files from anywhere or recover from a lost or broken computer.

Cloud storage

Keep and share files

Services like Google One, iCloud+, Dropbox and OneDrive keep selected files and photos online so you can open them from your phone, tablet or computer, and share them with other people.

Most sync changes across devices. That usually includes deletions, so a file removed on one device can disappear everywhere. Recovery windows for deleted files vary by plan.

Online backup

Recover from a failure

Services like Backblaze and IDrive copy what is on your computer automatically in the background, so you can restore it after theft, damage, a failed drive or ransomware.

Backup plans are priced differently: some offer unlimited storage for your computer, others sell a storage amount that can cover several computers.

Using both

Many households do

A common approach is one cloud storage plan for everyday access and sharing, plus a backup service for a full copy of the computer. Keeping a second copy somewhere else is the point of backup.

If you only need extra room for photos and phone backups, cloud storage alone may be enough.

Which do I need? If you mostly want more room for photos, documents and sharing, compare cloud storage. If your goal is protecting everything on a computer, compare backup. If both matter, compare both below.
Cloud storage

Compare cloud storage by size.

Plans are grouped by how much space you get and sorted by cost per terabyte per month, using the lower of the monthly and yearly standard price.

Checking storage price freshness…
How to read cost per terabyte. It divides the price by the storage (1 TB = 1,000 GB). Plans that bundle extras, such as AI features, Microsoft 365 apps or a VPN, cost more per terabyte because you are paying for more than storage. Shared plans only reach that cost if every person uses their share.
Online backup

Compare online backup plans.

Backup is priced differently from storage, so these plans are listed on their own. Cost per terabyte does not apply to unlimited plans.

Promotions are kept separate. First-year discounts, sales and lifetime offers are not used as the standard price. Where a provider advertises one, it is noted next to the plan.
