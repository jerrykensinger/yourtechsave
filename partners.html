Partnerships

Partner with YourTechSave

YourTechSave helps households understand recurring technology costs before they switch, cancel, upgrade or buy. We are building partnerships that can give readers useful next steps without compromising how comparisons are produced.

Consumer-intent audience

Readers arrive while reviewing wireless, internet, subscriptions, device protection and related household technology costs.

Measured actions

Google Analytics is configured to measure checkup starts, checkup completions, calculator use and outbound provider clicks without sending the financial values entered into the tools.

Editorial separation

Affiliate availability and commission amounts are kept outside recommendation criteria and provider-data verification.

Current publishing and comparison capabilities

YourTechSave currently includes a free Tech Spending Checkup, calculators, evergreen cost-saving guides, and source-linked wireless comparison pages. Provider records include official source URLs, a last-verified date, plan information and a freshness control that can hide time-sensitive pricing when records have not been rechecked.

Current wireless coverage includes major carriers and lower-cost alternatives, with comparison pages designed around plan structure, family pricing, network differences, hotspot, taxes and fees, and other practical switching considerations.

Partnership formats we are open to

Affiliate partnerships

Tracked provider or product links can be added to relevant comparison or guide pages when the partnership fits the reader's intent. Affiliate status is stored separately from editorial facts and ranking logic.

Direct referrals

Qualified local or specialized providers may be considered when they solve a clearly defined consumer need that YourTechSave does not directly provide.

Sponsored opportunities

Sponsored placements or content may be considered when clearly labeled and when the commercial relationship does not control independent comparison results.

What partners can expect

  • Clear affiliate and sponsored-content disclosures.
  • Official provider sources used for time-sensitive plan and pricing claims whenever practical.
  • Routine re-verification of provider comparison data.
  • No pay-to-win recommendation ordering.
  • Outbound provider-click measurement so partnership performance can be evaluated.
  • Corrections when a current factual claim is shown to be wrong.

What YourTechSave is not offering

We do not sell guaranteed rankings, positive reviews, or undisclosed endorsements. A commercial relationship does not guarantee inclusion in a comparison, and providers without affiliate programs can still be included when relevant.

Interested in working together?

Email partners@yourtechsave.com with the company name, program type, destination URL, compensation structure, tracking platform, and a contact person. For provider programs, include any current public pricing or plan-data feed that can help keep the comparison accurate.
