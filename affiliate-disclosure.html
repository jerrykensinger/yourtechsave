Affiliate Disclosure

Some links may result in compensation to YourTechSave. That commercial relationship is kept separate from the calculations and comparison criteria used on the site.

Updated October 3, 2026

When a link is an affiliate link

YourTechSave LLC may participate in affiliate or referral programs. If you click an eligible tracked link and complete a qualifying purchase, signup, activation or other action, YourTechSave may receive compensation under the partner program's terms.

How affiliate relationships affect recommendations

Affiliate availability and commission amount are not used as recommendation-ranking criteria. A provider may be included without paying YourTechSave, and a paying provider is not guaranteed favorable treatment or inclusion.

How affiliate links are labeled

When an outbound provider link is an affiliate link, YourTechSave intends to identify the relationship near the link or recommendation when practical. Technical link attributes may also identify sponsored or affiliate relationships to search engines.

Prices and eligibility

Provider prices, promotions, taxes, fees, eligibility requirements and program terms can change. YourTechSave maintains verification dates for time-sensitive comparison data, but the provider's own checkout and current terms control the transaction. Verify current terms directly with the provider before purchasing or switching service.

Sponsored content

If YourTechSave publishes sponsored content or a paid placement, it should be labeled as sponsored and kept distinct from independent comparison criteria.

Questions

Questions about an affiliate relationship or disclosure can be sent to partners@yourtechsave.com.
