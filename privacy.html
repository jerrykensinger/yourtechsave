Privacy Policy

YourTechSave is designed to collect less data than many comparison sites, especially inside the Tech Spending Checkup.

Updated October 10, 2026

Tech Spending Checkup

The current checkup performs its calculations in your web browser. It may load YourTechSave’s public wireless provider data file so it can compare your service-only estimate with fresh tracked standard pricing. That provider-data request does not include the dollar amounts, household answers, subscription names, or other values you enter. YourTechSave does not intentionally send those entered values to Google Analytics.

Home Internet Finder

The current Home Internet Finder runs in your browser. YourTechSave does not intentionally send the ZIP code, current internet cost, or other finder answers you enter to Google Analytics. Custom analytics events may record that the finder was completed and broad non-identifying preference categories such as connection type or priority.

Google Analytics

YourTechSave uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as page views and whether certain tools are started or completed. YourTechSave is intended for visitors in the United States. For these visitors, analytics is on by default and there is no consent banner. Google advertising features and Google signals are turned off for our measurement.

Google may receive technical and usage information associated with visiting the site, such as page information, browser or device information, approximate geographic information derived from network data, and interaction events. YourTechSave's custom events are designed to record actions such as a checkup start or completion, calculator use, and provider-link clicks without sending the financial values entered into the tools.

Opting out of analytics

If analytics is on for your browser, you can turn it off below.

We also respect the Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track browser signals: when either is set, analytics stays off. Visitors whose browser reports a European time zone also have analytics off by default. When analytics is off, our custom events and page views are not sent, although Google's tag may still send limited, cookieless technical pings that do not use analytics storage. Your choice is stored locally in your browser and can be reset by clearing site data for YourTechSave. Questions or requests can go to contact@yourtechsave.com.

Hosting logs

Hosting and network infrastructure may keep routine technical logs such as IP address, requested page, user agent and timestamps for security, reliability and operations.

External and affiliate links

When you follow a link to another company, that company's privacy practices apply. Some future provider links may contain affiliate or referral identifiers. Affiliate relationships are disclosed separately and do not change the arithmetic used in YourTechSave tools.

Email

If you email YourTechSave, we receive the information you choose to include in your message.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact contact@yourtechsave.com.
