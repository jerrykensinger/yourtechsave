Important: ZIP code alone cannot reliably prove that a provider serves your specific home. The FCC and many internet companies determine availability at the street-address level.

Why the finder separates “worth checking” from “available.”

Home internet is address-specific. Two houses on the same street can have different wired or fixed-wireless options. The finder prioritizes research paths; it does not label a company as available until you confirm the address with the FCC map or the provider.
