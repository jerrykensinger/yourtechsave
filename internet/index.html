Connection types

Start with the technology, then compare the provider.

Price matters, but the underlying connection can affect speed, latency, reliability, installation and availability.

Fiber

Usually the first wired option to investigate when available because it can support high download and upload speeds. Availability is highly local.

Cable

Widely used and often capable of high download speeds. Equipment, upload performance, promotional pricing and local competition can vary.

5G / fixed wireless

Uses a nearby cellular network instead of a wired line. Verizon and T-Mobile both require an address-level eligibility check because capacity can vary even within the same neighborhood.

Satellite

Can reach homes where wired service is limited. Modern low-Earth-orbit and traditional satellite systems have different latency, equipment and performance trade-offs.

Local provider lookup

Use an address-level source before assuming a company serves your home.

The FCC National Broadband Map lets a consumer enter an address and see providers that report service at that location, along with technology type and maximum advertised speeds. YourTechSave uses that as the preferred independent starting point for local provider discovery.
