Wireless savings calculator

What would a different cell phone plan actually save?

Compare service to service—not a full wireless bill to an advertised plan price. Enter recurring service costs only and keep phone financing separate.

Compare two service costs

Use service charges only. Do not include phone installments unless the alternative includes the same hardware cost. The amounts you enter stay in your browser and are not sent to Google Analytics.

Tip: If your current bill includes phone payments, insurance, watches or other add-ons, separate those first. The calculator is most useful when both numbers represent comparable recurring wireless service.
What to do with the result

Use the savings estimate as a filter, not a decision by itself.

After you see the difference, check coverage, hotspot, taxes and fees, device credits, international features and connected-device needs. A lower monthly price only helps when the service still fits the household.
