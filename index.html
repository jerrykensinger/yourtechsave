Frequently asked questions

What YourTechSave does — and does not do.

Is the Tech Spending Checkup free?

Yes. V1 does not require an account, and the checkup calculations run in your browser.

Does YourTechSave sell wireless or internet service?

No. YourTechSave is a comparison and education publisher. Some comparison pages link to third-party providers so visitors can verify current plans or investigate an option.

Are savings guaranteed?

No. The checkup starts with information you enter and can also use fresh, source-linked wireless plan records for a directional benchmark when comparable data is available. Provider pricing, eligibility, taxes and fees, coverage and service quality can still vary.

Can affiliate payments change a recommendation?

Affiliate availability and commission amounts are not used as recommendation-ranking criteria. Learn more on How We Make Money.
