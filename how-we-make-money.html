Business model

How YourTechSave makes money

The goal is to build a sustainable consumer resource without letting compensation decide the math or the recommendation order.

Updated October 3, 2026

Affiliate relationships

YourTechSave may earn compensation when a visitor follows certain tracked links and completes a qualifying purchase, signup or activation. When an affiliate link is used, the relationship is intended to be disclosed clearly enough that a reader can understand a commercial relationship exists before acting on the recommendation.

Our rule: affiliate availability and commission amount do not determine savings calculations, factual provider data, or recommendation ordering.

Sponsored placements

YourTechSave may accept clearly labeled sponsored placements or sponsored content in the future. Sponsored material should be identifiable as sponsored and should not be presented as an independent ranking or editorial conclusion.

Referral relationships

YourTechSave may also receive compensation for qualified referrals to businesses that provide services such as repair, installation, local technology support or other consumer solutions. Paid referral relationships should be disclosed where they are relevant.

Providers that do not pay us

A provider does not need to have an affiliate program to be included. The comparison database is intended to cover relevant consumer options even when there is no compensation available.

How affiliate links are kept separate from provider facts

Provider records store official source information, verification dates and plan facts separately from any affiliate destination. This allows a tracked link to be added or removed without rewriting the underlying provider comparison.

How success is measured

YourTechSave measures actions such as checkup completion and outbound provider clicks so we can understand whether the site is useful and whether a partnership produces engagement. The financial values entered into the Tech Spending Checkup are not intentionally sent to Google Analytics.

See the Affiliate Disclosure, Methodology and Partnerships page for more detail.
