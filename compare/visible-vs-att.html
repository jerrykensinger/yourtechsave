Visible vs. AT&T: simple per-line pricing vs. family-plan tiers

Visible uses straightforward per-line monthly pricing, while AT&T's current lineup uses multi-line family pricing with larger hotspot and premium-data benefits on higher tiers.

At a glance: Visible's standard monthly lineup is Visible, Visible+ and Visible+ Pro with taxes and fees included. AT&T currently offers Value 2.0, Extra 2.0, Premium 2.0 and Elite 2.0, advertised at $30, $40, $50 and $70 per line per month with 4 lines and qualifying AutoPay + paperless billing; taxes and fees extra.

The line count changes the comparison

Visible's standard price is straightforward on a per-line basis. AT&T's pricing is more dependent on the number of lines and selected tier. A four-line household should compare the total recurring account cost rather than multiplying a single-line advertisement.

Taxes and fees need to be compared on the same basis

Visible includes taxes and fees in its listed plan prices. AT&T adds taxes and fees to its advertised plan pricing. When estimating household savings, compare the amount that will actually recur on the bill.

Hotspot and premium features can justify different tiers

AT&T's higher tiers increase hotspot allowances and premium-data benefits. Visible's higher tiers also add benefits beyond the base plan. The useful question is whether your household actually uses those features enough to justify the higher recurring price.

Coverage should be tested independently

Visible and AT&T use different underlying networks. A lower price is not useful if coverage is materially worse at home, work, school or regular travel locations. Test the network experience before moving every line when possible.

Questions to answer before choosing

  • How many phone lines are on the account?
  • Which network performs better in the places that matter most?
  • How much hotspot data does each line need?
  • Are taxes and fees included in the advertised price?
  • Do you need watch, tablet or international benefits?
  • Would switching affect device financing or promotional credits?
