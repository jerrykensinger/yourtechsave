What this comparison does not do

YourTechSave does not automatically declare one provider better for everyone. Coverage experience, number of lines, device promotions, smartwatch needs, hotspot use, international travel and customer-service preferences can all change which option fits a household.

Why we exclude temporary promotions from the main comparison

Promotions can change quickly and often have eligibility rules, port-in requirements, device requirements or expiration dates. The comparison above focuses on standard pricing where a clean standard rate is available. A promotion can still be useful, but it should be evaluated separately and against its full terms.

Questions to answer before switching between Visible and Verizon

  • How many phone lines are on the account?
  • Are any phones still being financed?
  • Are promotional device credits still being received?
  • Do you need smartwatch, tablet or other connected-device lines?
  • How much hotspot data do you actually use?
  • Do you need international roaming or calling features?
  • Is your current Verizon coverage experience good in the places that matter?
Use the math first: calculate your current service cost per line, estimate family wireless savings, and review the carrier-switch checklist.
