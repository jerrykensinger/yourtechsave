AT&T vs. T-Mobile: compare the whole family plan

Both carriers use line-count pricing and offer premium data, hotspot, device promotions and international benefits. The useful comparison is the full household cost for the features you actually use.

At a glance: AT&T currently offers Value 2.0, Extra 2.0, Premium 2.0 and Elite 2.0. T-Mobile's current core lineup includes Essentials 2.0, Experience More 2.0 and Experience Beyond 2.0. Both use multi-line pricing and add taxes or fees outside the headline plan price.

Compare the total for the same number of lines

Both carriers use multi-line pricing, so a one-line advertisement is not a useful comparison for a four-line household. Compare the total recurring service cost for your actual line count, then add taxes, fees, watches, tablets and recurring extras.

Premium data and hotspot can justify a higher tier for some households

AT&T and T-Mobile both reserve larger hotspot allowances and more premium-data benefits for higher plan tiers. If a household rarely uses hotspot or heavy mobile data, paying for the top tier may not provide much practical value. Heavy users may see the comparison differently.

Entertainment and connected-device benefits are not free if you would not buy them

T-Mobile's Experience plans emphasize bundled entertainment and travel benefits. AT&T's higher tiers emphasize premium data, hotspot, international service and connected-device discounts. Treat a bundled benefit as savings only when it replaces something the household would otherwise pay for.

Device promotions can dominate the short-term math

Both carriers regularly advertise smartphone promotions with eligibility, financing and service requirements. Separate those temporary device incentives from the underlying service price so the long-term comparison remains clear.

Questions to answer before choosing

  • How many phone lines are on the account?
  • Which network is stronger at home, work, school and regular travel locations?
  • How much hotspot data does each line need?
  • Do you use the entertainment or international benefits included in premium tiers?
  • Are any device promotions or trade-in credits currently active?
  • What will the recurring bill be after taxes, fees and discounts?
