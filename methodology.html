Our Methodology

Show the math, separate facts from estimates, and never let affiliate payouts decide recommendation order.

What V1 calculates today

The Tech Spending Checkup uses values entered by the visitor to total monthly technology spending, annualize it, calculate wireless cost per line and identify the annual cost of rarely used subscriptions.

V1 does not claim a specific dollar amount can be saved by switching wireless or internet providers.

Future provider data

Provider records will include a source URL, last-verified date, standard price, promotion details, expiration date and major plan features. Expired promotions should not remain active in recommendations.

Recommendation order

Provider fit and consumer needs come first. Affiliate availability and commission amount are separate from recommendation criteria.
