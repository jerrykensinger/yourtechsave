How YourTechSave calculates and evaluates technology costs

The method is simple: separate the parts of a bill, show the math, label estimates, and keep affiliate compensation outside recommendation criteria.

Methodology version 1.1 • Updated October 3, 2026

What the V1 checkup calculates

The current checkup uses values entered by the visitor. It does not connect to bank accounts, carrier accounts, or subscription services. The main calculations are straightforward arithmetic.

Wireless service cost per line
Monthly wireless service cost ÷ number of phone lines paid for
Annual technology cost
Monthly recurring cost × 12
24-month cost
Monthly recurring cost × 24

Device financing is collected separately from wireless service so a phone payment is not accidentally presented as a plan expense that disappears when someone changes carriers.

What V1 does not claim

YourTechSave does not currently claim that switching a wireless or internet provider will save a specific dollar amount.

Those comparisons require current plan prices, taxes or fees where applicable, eligibility rules, promotions, service features and other conditions. Until a maintained provider-data process is in place, V1 limits dollar savings to calculations directly supported by visitor-entered values.

How future provider data will be handled

When provider comparisons are added, the intended record for each plan includes the official source URL, standard price, promotion details, promotion expiration, major plan features, last-verified date and review status. Time-sensitive offers should be rechecked before they are shown as current.

Recommendation criteria

Recommendations should consider the consumer's stated needs, including coverage experience, number of lines, desired features, internet usage and other relevant factors. Affiliate availability and commission amount are stored separately from those criteria and are not intended to determine ranking order.

Corrections

Technology pricing changes quickly. If you find a current factual error, email partners@yourtechsave.com. Material corrections should be reflected in the affected page rather than silently preserving an outdated claim.
