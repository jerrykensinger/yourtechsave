About YourTechSave

Helping households understand what technology really costs.

YourTechSave LLC is an independent Pennsylvania-based consumer technology savings company building self-service tools and practical guides for wireless, internet, subscriptions, devices and related services.

Meet the founder

JK

Jerry Kensinger

Founder & Owner, YourTechSave LLC • Harrisburg, Pennsylvania

I started YourTechSave after spending a lot of time untangling my own household’s technology bills, especially wireless. The hard part was not the arithmetic. It was separating service from device payments, understanding temporary promotions, and comparing plans that were structured differently.

That experience led to a simple idea: give people clear tools, show the source and verification date behind time-sensitive information, and make it completely acceptable when the best answer is to keep the service they already have.

contact@yourtechsave.com

Why YourTechSave exists

Household technology spending rarely arrives on one bill. Wireless service can be mixed with device financing. Streaming subscriptions renew on different dates. Internet prices can change after introductory periods. Protection plans may be billed through a carrier, retailer or manufacturer.

YourTechSave is designed to make those costs easier to see and compare before someone makes a change. The goal is not to tell every visitor to switch providers. Sometimes the useful answer is to keep a service because its coverage, reliability or features justify the cost.

What is live today

YourTechSave includes a free Tech Spending Checkup, simple cost calculators, practical guides, and a growing set of wireless provider comparisons. The checkup calculations rely on values entered by the visitor, while provider-comparison pages use source-linked plan records that are reviewed on a recurring schedule.

How provider data is handled

Time-sensitive wireless records include an official source, last-verified date and freshness controls. Temporary promotions are kept separate from standard pricing when possible so a short-lived offer does not silently become the site's long-term comparison baseline.

How trust is handled

YourTechSave separates arithmetic from recommendations, identifies when information is estimated, and does not use affiliate commission amounts as a recommendation-ranking factor. See the Methodology, Editorial Standards, Affiliate Disclosure, and How We Make Money.
