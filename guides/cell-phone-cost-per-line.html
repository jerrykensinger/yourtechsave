Wireless guide

Calculate your real cell phone cost per line

Start with service only

Separate phone, tablet and watch financing from wireless service. Then divide the service amount by the number of phone lines you pay for.

Example: $220 in service ÷ 4 lines = $55 per line. Device payments should not be counted as plan savings.

Then compare features

Coverage, data priority, hotspot, international use and smartwatch support can make plans materially different.
