Switching checklist

What to check before switching cell phone carriers

A cheaper plan can be attractive, but the cleanest switch happens when you check coverage, phone financing, unlocking and number transfer details first.

Published October 3, 2026 • YourTechSave wireless savings series

1. Check coverage where you actually spend time

Start with home, work, school, frequently visited stores or venues, and regular travel routes. Coverage maps are useful, but your own experience and local conditions matter. If possible, test the prospective network before moving every line.

2. Separate service cost from device payments

Know what portion of the current bill is wireless service and what portion is hardware financing. That prevents a phone installment from being mistaken for plan cost.

3. Find the remaining device balance and promotion terms

A carrier switch can affect promotional bill credits or financing arrangements. Read the current agreement and determine what, if anything, becomes due when service ends. Do not assume a phone becomes free simply because service is moved.

4. Confirm the phone can be used on the new service

Check both device compatibility and unlocking status. The FCC maintains consumer guidance on cell phone unlocking. Carrier-specific eligibility and timing can differ, so confirm the current provider's policy before the switch.

5. Gather the information needed to move your number

Number transfers generally require accurate account information. The FCC explains that consumers can keep an existing number when changing providers in qualifying circumstances and describes the porting process in its number portability guidance.

6. Do not forget secondary devices and features

Review smartwatch lines, tablets, hotspot needs, international use, visual voicemail, Wi‑Fi calling and any other features the household relies on. A low headline price may not include every feature you use today.

7. Understand activation, taxes and promotional pricing

Look beyond the first-month price. Check whether the rate is promotional, whether autopay is required, and whether taxes or fees are included or added. If a promotion expires, compare the regular price too.

8. Plan the timing of the switch

Keep access to the current account information until the transfer is complete and save copies of recent bills, device balances and promotion terms. Review the final bill after the move to confirm that service ended as expected.
