Device financing

What happens to your phone payments when you switch carriers?

Changing wireless service and paying for a phone are two different obligations. A service switch does not automatically erase an outstanding device balance.

Published October 3, 2026 • YourTechSave wireless savings series

Start by identifying who financed the phone

The phone may be financed through the carrier, the manufacturer, a retailer, a credit card or another lender. The effect of changing carriers depends on that agreement. Check the current balance and contract terms before starting a switch.

Promotional credits can be different from the device balance

Some carrier promotions spread credits across multiple monthly bills. If service ends early, future credits may stop even though the remaining device obligation continues. The exact result depends on the promotion and carrier terms, so review the offer details rather than assuming the advertised discount is fully earned on day one.

Why this matters when comparing savings

If a new plan is $60 per month cheaper but switching causes $600 of expected promotional credits to disappear, the first-year economics are different from the headline monthly savings. Treat one-time switching costs separately from recurring service savings.

First-year net change
(Monthly service difference × 12) − one-time switching costs or lost benefits

Check unlocking separately

Paying off a device and having it unlocked are related but not identical questions. Carrier unlocking policies vary. The FCC provides general consumer information about cell phone unlocking, and the current carrier should provide its specific eligibility rules.

A device-financing checklist before switching

  • Find the remaining phone balance.
  • Identify any monthly promotional bill credits.
  • Read what happens to those credits if service ends.
  • Confirm whether the phone must be paid off before it can be unlocked.
  • Check compatibility with the new provider.
  • Include any one-time payoff impact in your savings comparison.
